How to Smell Like a Pro

A quick primer so your tests feel intentional.

Top / Heart / Base

Fragrances unfold in three distinct layers. The top notes hit first—bright citrus or herbs that fade within 15 minutes. Heart notes emerge next, forming the fragrance's core character for 2-4 hours. Base notes anchor the scent, lingering on skin for 6+ hours with woods, musks, or resins.

Longevity

How long a fragrance lasts depends on concentration and your skin chemistry. Eau de Parfum typically lasts 6-8 hours, while Eau de Toilette fades after 3-5 hours. Test on pulse points where warmth amplifies projection—wrists, neck, inner elbows.

Sillage

Sillage measures the scent trail you leave behind. A fragrance with strong sillage announces your presence from across the room, while intimate sillage stays close to skin. Consider the occasion—boardroom meetings call for subtlety, evening events allow bold projection.

Tip: test once on skin and once on fabric to compare diffusion.

Common Questions

Clear answers, no friction.

What exactly is a decant?

A decant is a small portion of a full-size fragrance transferred into a travel-friendly vial. It lets you experience premium scents without committing to a full bottle, making luxury fragrances more accessible and affordable.

What size should I order?

We recommend starting with 5ml (approximately 50–70 sprays) to test a fragrance over several days. If you're already familiar with a scent and want it for travel or daily use, 10ml or 15ml options provide extended wear.

How should I store my decants?

Keep your decants in a cool, dark place away from direct sunlight and heat. Avoid storing them in bathrooms where humidity fluctuates. Proper storage preserves the fragrance's integrity and extends its lifespan.

How do I test a new fragrance properly?

Apply the decant to pulse points (wrists, neck) and allow it to develop over 6–8 hours. Fragrances evolve through top, heart, and base notes, so give it time to reveal its full character before deciding if it's right for you.

What if I have sensitive skin?

If you have sensitive skin or allergies, test a small amount on your inner wrist first. Wait 24 hours to check for any reaction. You can also spray on clothing instead of skin, though this may alter the scent's development slightly.

How do your recommendations work?

Our curated recommendations are based on fragrance families, note profiles, and seasonal suitability. We group scents by mood and occasion to help you discover new favorites that match your preferences and lifestyle.
